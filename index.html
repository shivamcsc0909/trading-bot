Methodologies

Forex Strategy Frameworks

Systematic technical models designed to analyze market structure and manage risk effectively.

Trend Following

Identifying structural momentum across higher timeframes to align entries with prevailing institutional direction and avoid counter-trend noise.

Breakout Trading

Capitalizing on consolidation expansions and volatility surges beyond established support and resistance boundaries with volume confirmation.

Price Action

Reading raw candlestick geometry, pin bars, engulfing patterns, and wick rejections without lagging indicator clutter.

Support & Resistance

Mapping liquidity pools, historical supply/demand clusters, and repeating round-number price block levels for high-probability setups.

Momentum Trading

Evaluating acceleration and exhaustion dynamics across European and US sessions to capture rapid intraday price shifts.

Risk-Based Management

Position sizing determined strictly by predefined pip stop distance and fixed equity percentage, eliminating emotional sizing errors.
